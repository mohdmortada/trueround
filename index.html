Read how levels work

Officials enter each judge's scores round by round, then sign the sheet. The result reaches both fighters' records as soon as it is signed.

Read who can change a record

Who can change a fighter's record?

Only the owner of each part. Fighters can ask for a check, never edit one.

What medical data goes on the record?

A status and an end date, with the doctor's licence. No diagnosis and no notes.

How do fighters under 18 join?

A guardian consents first. Junior profiles stay private, with no photo.

How do athlete levels work?

Five, from Foundation to Elite. Each event sets the lowest level it takes.
